About

I am an epidemiologist whose research focuses on how public policy, including poverty reduction and public health interventions, as well as social and environmental factors shape food security, nutrition, and health in Arctic communities.

I use mixed methods in my research and have longstanding partnerships with communities and multisectoral stakeholders.

I earned my PhD from the Yale School of Public Health, my Master of Public Health from Dartmouth College, and my Bachelor of Science in Biology (with a minor in Political Science) from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. I was a 2024-2026 Fulbright Arctic Initiative Scholar.
