About

Sappho Gilbert, PhD MPH I am an epidemiologist whose research focuses on the impacts of public policy (including poverty reduction and public health interventions) as well as social and environmental factors on Arctic communities' food security, nutrition, and wellbeing. I employ mixed methods in my research and enjoy longstanding community and multisectoral stakeholder partnerships.

I earned my PhD at the Yale School of Public Health, Master's in Public Health (MPH) at Dartmouth College, and Bachelor of Science (BS) in Biology (with a Minor in Political Science) from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
