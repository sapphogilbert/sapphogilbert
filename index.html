I am an epidemiologist who studies how public policy (including poverty reduction and public health interventions) and social and environmental factors shape food security, nutrition, and health in Arctic communities. In my research, I use mixed methods and have longstanding, collaborative partnerships with community-based and multisectoral stakeholders.

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow with the Arctic Initiative at the Harvard Kennedy School. Previously, I was a T32 Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health with appointments in the Departments of Nutrition and Environmental Health (where I remain an affiliate). In addition, I was a 2024-2026 Fulbright Arctic Initiative Scholar.

I earned my PhD from the Yale School of Public Health, my Master of Public Health from Dartmouth College, and my Bachelor of Science in Biology (with a minor in Political Science) from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
