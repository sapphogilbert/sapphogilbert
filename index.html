I am an epidemiologist who studies how public policy and social and environmental factors shape food security, nutrition, and health, with a primary focus on Arctic and Indigenous communities. In my research, I utilize mixed methods and have longstanding, collaborative partnerships with community-based and multisectoral stakeholders.

Currently, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow with the Arctic Initiative at the Harvard Kennedy School. Previously, I was a National Institutes of Health T32 Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, with appointments in the Departments of Nutrition and Environmental Health, where I remain an affiliate. In addition, I was a 2024–2026 Fulbright Arctic Initiative Scholar. My teaching experiences at Harvard Medical School and Yale University span epidemiology, food systems and nutrition, public health ethics, research ethics, and statistics.

I earned my PhD from the Yale School of Public Health, my Master of Public Health from Dartmouth College, and my Bachelor of Science in Biology (with a minor in Political Science) from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
