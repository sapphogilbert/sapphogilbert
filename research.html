Research

My research examines how food systems, environmental change, and public policy shape health in Arctic and Indigenous communities.

Arctic food systems and population health

I study how grocery access, household income, public benefits, community food programs, subsistence harvesting, and dietary change shape nutrition and health in Arctic communities.

Climate and environmental health

I investigate how climate and environmental change affect access to and safety of subsistence harvesting, food security, housing conditions, and health in the Arctic.

Community-engaged health policy and research methods

I utilize mixed methods and community-engaged approaches to produce research that is accountable to communities and useful for the design of health and food policy.

Supporting youth wellbeing & career development

I support research and mentorship focused on the wellbeing, leadership, education, and professional development of Arctic youth as well as that of students interested in Arctic and Indigenous research across the North.
